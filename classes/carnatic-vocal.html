Carnatic Vocal

Classical / Vocal

Explore Carnatic Vocal

Delve into the rich traditions of Carnatic music. Our vocal program focuses on the core principles of Sruti, Laya, and Raga mastery. From basic Sarali Varisais to advanced Krithi performances, we provide a deep, meditative learning environment for students of all ages.

Traditional Gurukul style learning
Mastery of Raga & Tala systems
Certification in Music Fine Arts
Regular concert opportunities
